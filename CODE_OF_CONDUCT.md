# Code of Conduct

Tiny11 GUI welcomes constructive participation from people of every background and experience level.

Be respectful, discuss technical decisions rather than attacking people, assume good faith while asking for evidence, and give contributors room to learn. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, publication of private information, and knowingly unsafe instructions are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when necessary to keep project spaces safe and useful. If a conduct problem cannot be handled safely in public, use the repository's private security-reporting channel and clearly mark the report as a conduct concern. Do not include unrelated vulnerability details.

By participating in this repository, you agree to follow these expectations in issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-managed spaces.
